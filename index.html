Research Overview

Tourism is no longer just leisure – it is a geospatial phenomenon. This study investigates tourism resources of Hyderabad City through a geographical lens, using GIS to map, measure, and manage sustainable tourism development.

Hyderabad, one of India's fastest-growing urban agglomerations, has witnessed a tourism boom that drives construction and growth. Without scientific planning, this growth risks damaging the very ecology it celebrates.

Why This Research Matters?

  • Research Gap: Very few comprehensive geospatial studies exist for Hyderabad's tourism system.
  • Ecological Risk: Unchecked construction around tourist hubs threatens water bodies.
  • GIS Advantage: Spatial mapping of tourist flow enables evidence-based policy.
  • Policy Impact: Useful for GHMC, Telangana Tourism, and planners.

Objectives

  1. To analyse geographical factors for tourism in Hyderabad.
  2. To assess impacts – spatial, social, economic, environmental.
  3. To evaluate role of government and private stakeholders.
  4. To propose sustainable tourism strategies using GIS.

GIS Methodology – Core Strength

• Spatial Database of 477 sites • Remote Sensing LULC & NDVI • Buffer Analysis 500m/1km/2km • Field Survey 400+ tourists • Tools: ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, GEE, SPSS
