What we study

Our Research Focus

Integrated interdisciplinary approaches to river and earth science research.
🌊

River Hydrology & Management

Studying river systems, flood resilience, sediment dynamics, watershed management and sustainable water-resource solutions across Indian river basins.

⛰️

Earth Systems & Geosciences

Examining geological processes, landforms, soil systems and landscape evolution to understand environmental change and earth-system dynamics.

🌱

Environmental Sciences

Exploring human–environment interactions, ecosystem pressures, environmental quality and pathways towards ecological resilience.

🛰️

GIS & Spatial Analysis

Applying geographic information systems, remote sensing and spatial methods to interpret changing landscapes and support research.

About MCRES

Understanding Earth.
Protecting Rivers.
Sharing Knowledge.

Mallaiah Center for River & Earth Sciences (MCRES) is an independent, non-governmental initiative focused on research, scientific understanding and knowledge dissemination in river and earth sciences.

MCRES brings together perspectives from geology, geography, hydrology and environmental science to study real-world challenges through field observations, research and public awareness.

Knowledge for a Sustainable Tomorrow

Our work connects scientific investigation with public understanding — so that rivers, landscapes and communities are studied, understood and protected together.

Research in action

Selected Research Initiatives

Connecting scientific investigation with the environmental and water challenges around us.
Research Framework · FRP-01

Integrated Assessment of the Musi River System

Research on river–urban interactions, flood vulnerability and environmental change in Hyderabad, with attention to the relationship between urban development and the river system.

Village Tank Research · VTR-01

Mella Duppalapally Tank Study

A field-oriented initiative focused on understanding a local tank system and its surrounding environmental context through observation and documentation.
