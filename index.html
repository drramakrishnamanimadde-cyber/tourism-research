About MCRES Understanding Earth. Protecting Rivers. Sharing Knowledge. Mallaiah Center for River & Earth Sciences (MCRES) is an independent, non-governmental initiative focused on research, scientific understanding and knowledge dissemination in river and earth sciences. MCRES brings together perspectives from geology, geography, hydrology and environmental science to study real-world challenges through field observations, research and public awareness. Knowledge for a Sustainable Tomorrow Research in action Selected Research Initiatives Connecting scientific investigation with the environmental and water challenges around us. Research Framework · FRP-01 Integrated Assessment of the Musi River System Research on river–urban interactions, flood vulnerability and environmental change in Hyderabad, with attention to the relationship between urban development and the river system. Village Tank Research · VTR-01 Mella Duppalapally Tank Study A field-oriented initiative focused on understanding a local tank system and its surrounding environmental context through observation and documentation.
