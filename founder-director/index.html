Dr. Ramakrishna Manimadde

Founder & Director, Mallaiah Center for River & Earth Sciences (MCRES)
Earth Scientist & River Systems Researcher

Dr. Ramakrishna Manimadde is an Earth Scientist and River Systems Researcher with an interdisciplinary academic background and a professional interest in understanding the interactions between rivers, landscapes, water resources, and human activities.

His work brings together perspectives from Geology, Geography, Hydrology, Environmental Sciences, and GIS-based spatial analysis. His primary interests include river systems, urban water challenges, environmental change, groundwater resources, and sustainable water management.

As the Founder and Director of the Mallaiah Center for River & Earth Sciences (MCRES), he works towards promoting scientific research, evidence-based understanding, and public awareness of Earth and river systems.

Professional Experience

He has professional experience in geological investigations, field surveys, and water-resource development projects through his work as a Geologist with WAPCOS.

His professional assignments included soil classification, field investigations, and spatial data collection associated with major irrigation and water-resource projects, including:

  • Pranahita–Chevella Sujala Sravanthi
  • Handri-Neeva Sujala Sravanthi
  • Galeru-Nagari Sujala Sravanthi
  • Dummugudem Project
  • Gouravelli Project
  • Velugodu Project
  • Srisailam Right Branch Canal (SRBC)
  • Telugu Ganga Project

These assignments provided practical exposure to geological field investigations, soil assessment, GNSS/HNSS-based field surveys and spatial data collection, contributing to his understanding of water-resource development and field-based environmental assessments.

Research Initiatives & Science Communication

1. Integrated Assessment of the Musi River System (MCRES-FRP-01) ONGOING RESEARCH INITIATIVE
An interdisciplinary research initiative examining river–urban interactions, flood vulnerability, and environmental change in the Musi River system, with particular attention to the Hyderabad urban corridor.

2. Mella Duppalapally Tank Study (MCRES-VTR-01) FIELD-BASED RESEARCH INITIATIVE
A field-based research initiative focused on understanding the local tank system, its environmental characteristics, and its significance for local water resources.

3. Science Communication
He contributes to public scientific awareness through science-oriented articles and knowledge dissemination, particularly on river systems, groundwater resources, environmental issues, and the relationship between society and natural systems.

About MCRES

Mallaiah Center for River & Earth Sciences (MCRES) is an independent, non-governmental research initiative established in memory of the founder's father, Mallaiah.

The institution aims to promote scientific research, interdisciplinary understanding, and public awareness in Earth Sciences and River Systems, with particular attention to rivers, water resources, environmental processes, and sustainable management.

As its Founder and Director, Dr. Ramakrishna Manimadde provides the institution's research direction and intellectual leadership.
