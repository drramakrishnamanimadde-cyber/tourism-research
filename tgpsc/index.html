📝 TGPSC OTR Registration - 5 Easy Steps

1

Visit Official Site: Go to tgpsc.gov.in → Click on OTR - NEW REGISTRATION

2

New Registration: Enter Name, DOB, Mobile, Email → Get TGPSC ID via SMS/Email

3

Login & Fill: Login with TGPSC ID + DOB + OTP → Fill Education, Address

4

Apply Notification: Select Group-1,2,3,4 Notification → Upload Photo & Signature (JPG)

5

Pay & Download: Pay Fee Online (₹200-₹350) → Download Application PDF

📚 Notifications

🎓 Qualification
