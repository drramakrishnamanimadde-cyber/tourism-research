📝 TGPSC OTR Steps

1

Visit: Go to tgpsc.gov.in → Click OTR NEW REGISTRATION

2

Register: Fill Name, Mobile, Email → Get TGPSC ID

3

Login: Login with TGPSC ID + DOB + OTP

4

Apply: Select Group-1,2,3,4 → Upload Photo

5

Pay: Pay Fee & Download Application
