Project Overview

MCRES-VTR-01 is a village-level research initiative focused on the tank system of Village : Mella Duppalapally / గ్రామం : మేళ్ళ దుప్పలపల్లి, Mandal & District : Nalgonda / మండలం & జిల్లా : నల్లగొండ, Telangana / తెలంగాణ.

The project aims to develop a systematic understanding of the tank through geographical assessment, water-resource research, environmental observation, historical evidence collection, and field-based investigation.

At the initial stage, the project focuses on identifying reliable sources of information and establishing a baseline for further research. No unverified figures or historical claims are treated as established facts.

Research Objectives

1. To identify the geographical location and physical characteristics of the village tank - Village : Mella Duppalapally / గ్రామం : మేళ్ళ దుప్పలపల్లి
2. To investigate the tank's water inflow and outflow features - Mandal & District : Nalgonda / మండలం & జిల్లా : నల్లగొండ
3. To collect and examine available historical records, maps, and other documentary evidence.
4. To document observable environmental conditions and changes over time.
5. To establish a research database that distinguishes verified information, field observations, and estimates.
6. To prepare a scientific report based on documented evidence and transparent research methods.
🔬 Current Research Status: Initial documentation and evidence-collection stage
