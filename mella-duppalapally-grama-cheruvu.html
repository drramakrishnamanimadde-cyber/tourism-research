Project Overview

MCRES-VTR-01 is a village-level research initiative focused on the tank system of Village : Mella Duppalapally / గ్రామం : మేళ్ళ దుప్పలపల్లి, Mandal & District : Nalgonda / నల్లగొండ, Telangana.

The project aims to develop a systematic understanding of the tank through geographical assessment, water-resource research, environmental observation, historical evidence collection, and field-based investigation.

No unverified figures or historical claims are treated as established facts - Pure Research Only.

Research Methodology

The study will use available documentary records, maps, satellite imagery, field observations, and local information, subject to availability and verification. Measurements and estimates will be clearly distinguished.

Research Objectives - 3D Cards

01To identify the geographical location and physical characteristics of the village tank.
02To investigate the tank's water inflow and outflow features and surrounding landscape.
03To collect and examine available historical records, maps, and documentary evidence.
04To document observable environmental conditions and changes over time, wherever reliable evidence is available.
05To establish a research database that distinguishes verified information, field observations, and estimates.
06To prepare a scientific report based on documented evidence and transparent research methods.
