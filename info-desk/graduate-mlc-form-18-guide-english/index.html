Graduate MLC Voter Registration - Form 18 Complete Guide (English)

Official Source: ceotelangana.nic.in | ECI Notification - September 2024

1. Eligibility:

  • Must be ordinarily resident in Telangana Graduate Constituency
  • Must have completed Degree before 01-11-2023 (3 years before qualifying date 01-11-2026)
  • For Mahabubnagar-Rangareddy-Hyderabad & Warangal-Khammam-Nalgonda - Fresh Electoral Roll 2026

2. Documents Required:

  • Degree Certificate / Provisional / Marks Memo Xerox Copy - 1
  • Passport Size Photo - 1
  • Aadhaar Card, Address Proof - 1

Note: No Attestation Required - Only Xerox Copies Enough. Gazetted Officer / Notary Sign NOT Required - As per latest ERO guidelines.

3. How to Apply:

Online Method (Best): Fill Form 18 on ceotserms2.telangana.gov.in and upload documents.

Offline Method: Take print of Form 18 and submit at ERO / AERO / Tahsildar / MRO Office or send by post.
