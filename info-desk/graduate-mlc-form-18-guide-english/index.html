Graduate MLC Voter Registration - Form 18 Complete Guide (English)

Official Source: ceotelangana.nic.in | ECI Notification - September 2026

1. Eligibility

  • Must be an ordinarily resident of Telangana Graduate Constituency
  • Must have graduated at least 3 years before qualifying date 01-11-2026 (Degree completed before 01-11-2023)
  • Applicable for Mahabubnagar-Rangareddy-Hyderabad & Warangal-Khammam-Nalgonda - Fresh Electoral Roll 2026

2. Documents Required

  • Degree Certificate / Provisional / Marks Memo attested by Gazetted Officer / Notary
  • Passport Size Photo
  • Aadhaar Card, Address Proof

3. How to Apply

Online Method (Best): Fill Form 18 on ceotserms2.telangana.gov.in and upload documents.

Offline Method: Take print of Form 18 and submit at ERO / AERO / Tahsildar / MRO Office or send by post.
