Graduate MLC Voter Registration – Form 18 Complete Guide (Telangana)

Source: CEO Telangana Guidelines (ceotelangana.nic.in) | Prepared by: Dr Ramakrishna Manimadde, Director, MCRES

Important: This is an informational awareness guide only. It is not an official government notification. Please verify latest details on CEO Telangana official website.

1. What is Graduate MLC?

Graduate MLC (Member of Legislative Council) is elected by graduate voters of a Council Constituency. In Telangana, Graduate Constituencies include Hyderabad, Rangareddy, Mahabubnagar etc.

2. Eligibility Criteria

  • Must be an Indian citizen and ordinary resident of the constituency.
  • Must have completed graduation from any recognized university before 01-11-2023 (for current revision).
  • Must have completed 3 years after graduation.
  • Must not be registered as voter in any other Graduate Constituency.
  • Age: Usually 18+ but graduate condition applies.

3. Documents Required for Form 18

  • Degree Certificate / Provisional + Marks Memos (Proof of Graduation)
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Address Proof: Voter ID / Residence Certificate / Passport / Driving License
  • Passport Size Photo
  • Mobile Number & Email
  • Existing EPIC No. (if you have normal voter ID)

4. How to Apply Online – Step by Step

  1. Visit official website: ceotelangana.nic.in
  2. Click on E-Registration → Council Constituency → Form 18
  3. Fill personal details, address, educational qualification details
  4. Upload scanned copies of Degree & Address Proof (PDF/JPG under 2MB)
  5. Verify and Submit – You will get Reference Number
  6. BLO / Tahsildar office will verify and final electoral roll will be published

5. Offline Method

Download Form 18 from CEO Telangana site, fill manually, attach self-attested xerox copies, and submit at Concerned Tahsildar Office / Designated Location or to BLO.

6. Important Dates & Validity

Registration is done every 6 years. Once registered, you need to re-register in next revision cycle. Keep checking CEO Telangana notifications for last date.

7. Official Sources to Verify

  • ceotelangana.nic.in – Official Website
  • CEO Telangana Voter Helpline: 1950
  • Your District Tahsildar Office
