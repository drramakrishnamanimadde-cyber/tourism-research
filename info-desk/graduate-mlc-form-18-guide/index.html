Graduate MLC Voter Registration – Form 18 Complete Guide (Telangana) - 2027 Elections

Source: CEO Telangana (ceotelangana.nic.in) & Official Notice Dt: 28-09-2025 | Prepared by: Dr. Ramakrishna Manimadde, MCRES

⚠️ Important - Fresh Enrolment Mandatory: This time a completely fresh electoral roll is being prepared. Even if you were already enrolled as Graduate Voter earlier, you must apply again with Form-18. Old list will NOT be carried forward.
Official MLC 2026 Page Direct Form-18 Apply Link

1. What is Graduate MLC?

Graduate MLC (Member of Legislative Council) is elected by graduate voters of a Graduate Constituency. In Telangana, elections are due in March 2027 for Mahabubnagar-Rangareddy-Hyderabad and Warangal-Khammam-Nalgonda Graduates Constituencies.

2. Eligibility Criteria (As per Latest Revision 2026)

  • Must be an Indian citizen and ordinarily resident in the constituency.
  • Must have completed graduation BEFORE 01-11-2023 (Qualifying Date is 01-11-2026, so 3 years before that).
  • Candidates who graduated in 2023 or earlier are eligible.
  • Fresh Enrolment Mandatory: Existing voters must also re-apply with Form-18. Old roll is invalid (CEO Notice 28-09-2025).
  • Must not be registered in any other Graduate Constituency.

3. Documents Required for Form 18

  • Degree Certificate / Provisional + Marks Memos - Duly attested by Gazetted Officer / Designated Officer / Notary Public after verification with original
  • Aadhaar Card, Passport Size Photo
  • Address Proof: Voter ID / Residence Certificate / Passport
  • Mobile Number, Email, Existing EPIC No. of Assembly Constituency
  • For Govt Employees: Certificate from Gazetted Head of Office

4. How to Apply Online – Step by Step

  1. Visit official website: ceotelangana.nic.in/Mlc2026.html
  2. Go to E-Registration → Council Constituency → Form-18 (Graduates)
  3. Fill personal details, address, qualification details
  4. Upload scanned copies (attested) – PDF/JPG under 2MB
  5. Submit and note Reference Number for tracking
  6. Verification by BLO / Tahsildar office

5. Offline Method

Download Form-18 from CEO Telangana site, fill manually, attach attested copies and submit at concerned Tahsildar Office / Designated Location or to BLO.

6. Important Dates & Validity - For 2027 Elections (Latest & Verified)

  • Qualifying Date: 01-11-2026
  • Fresh Roll Preparation Started: 28-09-2025
  • Last Date for Form-18: 06-11-2026
  • Draft Roll Publication: 25-11-2026
  • Final Roll Publication: 30-12-2026
  • Election Due: March 2027 (Mahabubnagar-Rangareddy-Hyderabad & Warangal-Khammam-Nalgonda)
  • Validity: Fresh roll this time. Next revision after 6 years.
