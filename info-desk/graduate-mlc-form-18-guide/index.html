Graduate MLC Voter Registration – Form 18 Complete Guide (Telangana) - 2027 Elections

Source: CEO Telangana (ceotelangana.nic.in) & Official Notice Dt: 28-09-2025 | Prepared by: Dr. Ramakrishna Manimadde, MCRES

⚠️ Important - Fresh Enrolment / Re-enrolment Required: This time a completely fresh electoral roll is being prepared. Even if you were already enrolled as Graduate Voter earlier, you must apply again with Form-18. Old list will NOT be carried forward.
Official MLC 2026 Page Direct Form-18 Apply Link

1. What is Graduate MLC?

Graduate MLC is elected by graduate voters. In Telangana, elections are due in March 2027 for Mahabubnagar-Rangareddy-Hyderabad and Warangal-Khammam-Nalgonda Graduates Constituencies.

2. Eligibility Criteria (As per Latest Revision)

  • Must have completed graduation at least 3 years before 01-11-2026 (i.e., on or before 01-11-2023)
  • Must fulfil the eligibility requirements for the concerned Graduates' Constituency
  • Fresh enrolment / re-enrolment through Form-18 is required for all eligible graduates - Even if enrolled earlier, you must apply again. Old roll will not be carried forward.

3. Documents Required for Form 18

  • Degree Certificate / Provisional + Marks Memos - Duly attested by Gazetted Officer / Designated Officer / Notary Public
  • Aadhaar Card, Passport Size Photo, Address Proof, Mobile, Email, Existing EPIC No.

4. How to Apply Online

  1. Visit: ceotelangana.nic.in/Mlc2026.html
  2. E-Registration → Council Constituency → Form-18 (Graduates)
  3. Fill details, upload attested documents, Submit & note Reference No.

5. Offline Method

Download Form-18 and submit at Tahsildar Office / Designated Location or to BLO with attested copies.

6. Important Dates & Schedule - For 2027 Elections (Verified)

  • Qualifying Date: 01-11-2026
  • Last Date for Submission of Form-18 Applications: 06-11-2026
  • Draft Electoral Roll Publication: 25-11-2026
  • Claims & Objections Period: 25-11-2026 to 10-12-2026
  • Disposal of Claims & Objections: by 25-12-2026
  • Final Electoral Roll Publication: 30-12-2026
  • Elections: Expected in March 2027, subject to official election schedule to be notified by ECI
