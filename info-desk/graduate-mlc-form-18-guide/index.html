Our Information Standards & Editorial Policy

MCRES Info Desk Working Standard | Effective from: 06 Oct 2025 | Prepared & Published by: Dr. Ramakrishna Manimadde, MCRES

Our Commitment: MCRES Info Desk is not just an information-sharing page. We aim to be a source-based, responsible, and credible public-information platform for Telangana citizens.

1. Official Source Cross-Check

Every informational post related to Eligibility, Dates, Rules, and Procedures is cross-checked with official sources such as:

  • Election Commission of India (ECI)
  • Chief Electoral Officer, Telangana (ceotelangana.nic.in)
  • District Official Portals / Government Orders / Official Press Notes

2. Precise Wording

Where legal / eligibility conditions are involved, we use wording close to the official notification. Example:

Instead of "Graduation before 2023", we write "Must have completed graduation at least 3 years before 01-11-2026 (i.e., on or before 01-11-2023)" - as per ECI norm.

3. Fact vs Interpretation

We clearly separate:

  • Official Fact: What the official notification says
  • Our Explanation: Simplified awareness information for public understanding

4. Responsible Wording for Future Events

When official election schedule / notification is not yet issued, we avoid definitive claims. We use responsible phrasing:

  • "Expected in March 2027"
  • "Subject to official election schedule to be notified by ECI"
  • "Based on official sources available as of [Date]"

We never use "100% Final" or "100% Guaranteed" in public posts. "100% Verified" is our internal quality standard.

5. Source Mention is Mandatory

Every post includes: Source: ECI / CEO Telangana / District Notice + Link to official website for verification.

6. No Unsupported Claims

We strictly avoid:

  • Unconfirmed poll dates
  • Political predictions or endorsements
  • Guarantees without official backing

7. MCRES Branding & Identity

  • Top Bar: MALLAIAH CENTER FOR RIVER & EARTH SCIENCES (MCRES) - INFO DESK
  • Info Bar: Informational Guide • Non-Governmental • Awareness Purpose Only
  • Footer: Prepared & Published by Dr. Ramakrishna Manimadde, MCRES | For awareness purpose only. Please verify on official website.

8. Disclaimer

MCRES Info Desk is a non-governmental informational initiative. While we strive for accuracy through official source verification, users are advised to always verify with the concerned official department website (e.g., ceotelangana.nic.in) before taking action.

This standards page defines our working methodology to build long-term credibility as a responsible public-information platform.
