📖 What is UGC NET?

UGC NET is national exam for college teaching jobs. Qualified candidates eligible for Assistant Professor in 83 subjects. Top 6% get JRF with Rs.37,000 scholarship. Details on ugcnet.nta.nic.in and UGC Official Website.

🎓 Eligibility Criteria

Master's Degree 55% (50% for reserved), Final year PG students can also apply, No age limit for Asst Professor, JRF Max 30 years + 5 years relaxation. Full info: Download Information Bulletin PDF

💰 Application Fee & Syllabus

Gen: Rs.1150, OBC: Rs.600, SC/ST/PwD: Rs.325. Exam: 2 Papers (Paper 1 - 100 marks, Paper 2 - 200 marks). No negative marking.
