Welcome to MCRES: MCRES is a research centre committed to tribal development, rural livelihoods, environmental sustainability and responsible tourism research across India.

About Founder

Dr. R. Krishna Manimadde is the Founder Director of MCRES (Malavathu Centre for Rural and Environmental Studies). With deep commitment to rural communities, he established MCRES to promote research in tourism, rural development and environmental studies.

He is S/o Late Shri. R. Venkataiah, whose values of service and integrity continue to guide the mission of MCRES.

Vision

To become a leading research centre for sustainable rural development, tribal empowerment and responsible tourism that balances livelihoods with environmental conservation.

Mission

To conduct high-quality research, document grassroots knowledge, support rural communities, and provide policy inputs for sustainable development in Nellore, Andhra Pradesh and beyond.

Focus Areas

✔ Tribal & Rural Livelihoods
✔ Sustainable & Responsible Tourism
✔ Environmental Conservation
✔ Community Based Research & Documentation
✔ Rural Entrepreneurship Development
