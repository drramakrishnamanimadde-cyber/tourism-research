Mallaiah Center for River System & Earth Sciences (MCRES)

1

Institutional Vision

Mallaiah Center for River System & Earth Sciences (MCRES) aims to develop into a multidisciplinary research and scientific knowledge institution dedicated to understanding river systems, Earth processes, environmental change, and their interactions with human society.

MCRES will combine field-based research, scientific analysis, geospatial approaches, evidence synthesis, scientific documentation, education, and public science communication to generate knowledge that contributes to sustainable management of rivers, landscapes, water resources, and the environment.

2

Institutional Mission

🌊

Conduct research on river systems and their associated landscapes.

🪨

Study geological, geomorphological, hydrological and environmental processes.

📊

Document the changing condition of rivers, lakes, wetlands and watersheds.

🏙️

Examine the relationship between urbanisation, land-use change, water systems and environmental risk.

📄

Produce scientifically credible reports, research papers, datasets and knowledge resources.

🤝

Promote interdisciplinary collaboration between Earth sciences, geography, environmental sciences and allied disciplines.

🎓

Provide research opportunities, internships and scientific training for students and early-career researchers.

📢

Translate scientific knowledge into accessible information for policymakers, institutions and the public.

🌐

Build long-term collaborations with universities, research institutions, government agencies and other scientific organisations.
